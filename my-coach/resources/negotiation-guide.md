# Guide: The Customer Negotiation Round

The customer pushes back: more scope, an impossible date, "just make it work."

**What great looks like**
1. **Clarify the real goal** — what outcome the date or feature protects.
2. **Decompose the ask** — split must-haves from nice-to-haves; offer a
   smaller first deliverable that hits the goal on time.
3. **Name the risk** — say plainly what breaks if you skip testing or rush.
4. **Trade, don't cave** — scope vs. time vs. quality: pick two, say no to the
   third, and offer an alternative so trust holds.
5. **Confirm in writing** — recap scope, date, and owners in a follow-up.

**Anti-patterns:** saying yes to everything; a flat no with no option.
