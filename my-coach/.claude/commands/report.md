---
description: Summarise interview-practice results into a short progress report.
---

Read `data/results.json` and produce a short progress report with:

- how many rounds have been practised
- the average total score (out of 20)
- the weakest rubric category across all attempts (lowest average, using each
  record's `categories` scores; skip older records that don't have them)
- one concrete thing to focus on next

Keep it to a few lines. If there are no results yet, say so and suggest running
a mock round first. Don't modify any files.
